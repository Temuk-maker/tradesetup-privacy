Privacy Policy

TradeSetup — what gets measured, gets accomplished

Last updated: 21 October 2026

In short: your journal lives on your phone. TradeSetup sends data off your phone only when you choose to connect a MetaTrader 5 account, buy TradeSetup Pro, or send us feedback. We never sell your data, show ads, or track you.

What stays on your phone

Everything you create in TradeSetup is written to the app's own private storage on your phone:

  • Daily profit and loss, trade counts, notes, and the trades synced from MetaTrader 5
  • Per-trade details: profit or loss, entry time, holding time, the confirmation chosen, the mistake recorded
  • Images you attach, and any text you draw on them
  • Your rules, and which rules you marked as broken on a given day
  • Your saved lists of confirmations, mistakes, patterns and observations
  • App preferences such as the theme and Pro mode

We do not keep a copy of your journal. No other app can read it.

Connecting MetaTrader 5 (optional, TradeSetup Pro)

To fill your journal automatically, you can connect a MetaTrader 5 account with its investor (read-only) password. That password can see your trades but cannot place trades or move money.

  • What you enter: the account number, the investor password and the broker's server name.
  • Where it goes: over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to TradeSetup's sync service, hosted on Cloudflare. The sync service registers the account with MetaApi, a third-party service that connects to MetaTrader 5 servers. MetaApi keeps the account number, investor password and server so it can read your closed trades and balance.
  • What our sync service keeps: the account number and server, MetaApi's id for the account, a salted one-way hash of the investor password (never the password itself), when the account was connected and last synced, and a random id for each phone connected to it. That id is a SHA-256 hash of a key created on your phone; it does not identify you.
  • Your trades are not stored on our servers. Closed trades and the account balance are passed straight to your phone and kept only there.

Buying TradeSetup Pro

Payments are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. RevenueCat checks whether your subscription is active. It receives your purchase history, the same random phone id described above, and basic technical information about your device needed to validate purchases.

Sending feedback

If you send feedback from the app, we receive your message, any images you attach, whether your phone runs iOS or Android, and the app version. It is delivered to the developer by email through Google Apps Script. Your name or email address is not included unless you write it in the message.

Photos and files you choose

Adding an image to a day or a trade opens your phone's own photo picker. Only the image you pick is copied into the app's private storage. Where the app offers to import a MetaTrader report file, only the file you pick is read, once, on your phone. The app does not browse or scan your photos or files.

What we do not do

  • No accounts. You do not register, sign in, or give us an email address.
  • No analytics or tracking. No usage measurement, no advertising id, no third-party analytics or crash-reporting software.
  • No advertising.
  • No selling or sharing. Your data goes only to the service providers named above, and only to run the features you use.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your phone: deleting TradeSetup removes everything it stored. Your journal is not backed up to our servers, so it is gone unless it is in your own phone backup.
  • MetaTrader 5 connection: Disconnect in the app stops syncing on that phone. The account record and its MetaApi connection are kept, so connecting again later works straight away. To delete them completely, including from MetaApi, email us the account number and server, and we will delete them within 30 days.
  • Purchases: Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records as needed for billing and the law.

Children

TradeSetup is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.
