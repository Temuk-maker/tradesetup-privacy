Privacy Policy

TradeSetup — what gets measured, gets accomplished

Last updated: 23 August 2026

In short: TradeSetup does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no advertising.

What the app stores

Everything you create in TradeSetup is written to the app's own private storage on your device:

  • Daily profit and loss figures, trade counts and notes
  • Per-trade entries: profit/loss, the confirmation chosen, the mistake recorded
  • Images you attach, and any text you draw on them
  • Your rules, and which rules you marked as broken on a given day
  • Your saved lists of confirmations, mistakes, patterns and observations
  • App preferences such as Pro mode

This data never leaves your device. No other app can read it.

What the app does not do

  • No network access. The released Android app declares no internet permission, so it is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere.
  • No accounts. You do not register, sign in, or provide an email address.
  • No analytics or tracking. No usage measurement, no advertising ID collection, no third-party analytics or crash-reporting software.
  • No advertising.
  • No data sharing or selling. There is nobody to share it with.

Photos and files you choose

Two features open your device's own pickers:

  • Adding an image to a day or a trade uses the Android photo picker. Only the image you pick is copied into the app's private storage.
  • Automatic journal uses the Android file picker to read a MetaTrader 4/5 account history report (CSV or HTML) that you select. The file is read once, its trade rows are written into your journal, and no copy of the original file is kept.

In both cases the app can only see the specific item you choose. It does not browse or scan your gallery or storage, and it requests no storage permissions.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed. To delete all of it, uninstall TradeSetup, or go to Android Settings → Apps → TradeSetup → Storage → Clear storage. Because nothing is stored off the device, deletion is immediate and complete.

This also means your journal is not backed up to any server. If you lose or reset your device, the data is gone unless it was included in your own Android backup.

Children

TradeSetup is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
